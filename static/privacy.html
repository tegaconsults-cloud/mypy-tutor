MYPY TUTOR

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 1 October 2025  ·  Last Updated: 1 October 2026

Teamsamikoko Global Academy (Reg No: 3508656)

1. Data We Collect

  • Account data: name, email address, hashed password.
  • Learning data: topics studied, XP earned, quiz results, course progress, assignments.
  • Payment data: amount paid, plan purchased, Paystack transaction reference. We never store card numbers or CVVs.
  • Referral data: referral codes used, referral earnings, withdrawal requests.
  • Technical data: IP address (for rate-limiting), browser type, device type.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Deliver personalised AI tutoring and track your learning progress.
  • Process payments and issue receipts and certificates.
  • Administer the referral programme and process withdrawal requests.
  • Send transactional emails (confirmations, receipts, re-engagement reminders).
  • Improve the Platform through aggregated, anonymised analytics.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Paystack — payment processing (subject to Paystack's own privacy policy).
  • Supabase — cloud database hosting (data stored in EU-West region).
  • Resend / SMTP — transactional email delivery.
  • Groq — AI inference (your messages are processed to generate tutor responses; Groq's data retention policies apply).
  • Law enforcement or regulatory bodies, when required by Nigerian law.

4. Data Retention

We retain your account and learning data for as long as your account is active. If you request account deletion, all personal data is permanently removed within 30 days, except data we are legally required to retain (e.g. payment records for tax purposes, retained for 7 years under Nigerian FIRS requirements).

5. Your Rights (NDPR)

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) 2019, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data (right to erasure).
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, use the Support/Enquiry form on the Platform.

6. Security

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. All data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS. Database access is restricted to the application server. We conduct regular security reviews. Despite these measures, no system is completely immune to breach; we will notify affected users within 72 hours of discovering a material data breach.

7. Cookies

The Platform uses only functional cookies (session management, authentication tokens stored in localStorage). We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version is always at mypytutor.com.ng/privacy. Material changes will be communicated by email.

9. Contact

Privacy questions: Support/Enquiry form at mypytutor.com.ng. Data Protection Officer: Amb. Samuel Atulegwu Nwosu (Sir. Tega) — Teamsamikoko Global Academy.
